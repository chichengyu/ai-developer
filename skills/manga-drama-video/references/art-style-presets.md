## 1. Cyberpunk neon (赛博朋克)

**Global style statement**
Cinematic widescreen 16:9 illustration in a saturated cyberpunk anime style. Heavy use of volumetric neon (magenta, cyan, electric blue) against deep midnight blacks. Wet asphalt reflections, holographic signage, and rain streaks. Line work is clean and confident; rim lighting separates characters from the busy background. Lens: 35mm anamorphic feel with subtle bloom and chromatic aberration.

**Color palette**
- #0B0B1F — Midnight base
- #FF2E63 — Neon magenta
- #08D9D6 — Cyan glow
- #F9F871 — Sodium highlight
- #252A34 — Concrete shadow

**Do / Don't**
- Do: widescreen 16:9, neon rim light, rain reflections, dense signage
- Don't: 3D render, photoreal faces, daylight, chibi proportions

## 2. Ink wash / 古风水墨

**Global style statement**
Vertical-scroll inspired Chinese ink-wash illustration with cinematic 16:9 framing. Sumptuous negative space, flowing brush strokes, and restrained color washes over a warm rice-paper texture. Characters wear flowing hanfu with silk folds caught in implied wind. Lighting is diffuse and atmospheric; small flecks of vermilion seal accents punctuate otherwise muted palettes.

**Color palette**
- #F4ECD8 — Rice paper
- #1A1A1A — Ink black
- #A23E48 — Vermilion seal
- #5A6E7A — Distant mountain
- #C6A969 — Gilded accent

**Do / Don't**
- Do: large negative space, ink gradients, silk folds, subtle seal stamps
- Don't: hard cel shading, neon, photoreal skin, English signage

---

## 3. Healing hand-drawn / 治愈手绘

**Global style statement**
Soft hand-drawn illustration in warm pastel tones, picture-book vibe but kept cinematic 16:9. Loose pencil-like line work, watercolor fills with visible paper grain, and gentle natural light (golden hour, soft window light). Small floating motifs (leaves, sparkles, petals) add texture without crowding.

**Color palette**
- #FFF7E8 — Cream paper
- #F6BD60 — Honey
- #F7EDE2 — Blush
- #84A59D — Sage
- #5B5F97 — Dusk blue

**Do / Don't**
- Do: pastel palette, soft shadows, hand-drawn imperfections
- Don't: harsh contrast, anime screentone, photoreal faces

## 4. Shonen hot-blooded / 日漫热血

**Global style statement**
High-impact shonen action manga style with bold black line work, dramatic speed lines, and saturated primary colors. Dynamic foreshortening, expressive faces, big readable silhouettes. Cinematic 16:9 framing; background detail dense but clear. Energy effects use sharp white highlights on solid color.

**Color palette**
- #FFFFFF — Impact white
- #111111 — Manga black
- #E63946 — Hero red
- #F4A261 — Energy orange
- #1D3557 — Deep focus blue

**Do / Don't**
- Do: speed lines, halftone shading, exaggerated perspective, bold silhouettes
- Don't: soft watercolor, photoreal faces, chibi SD proportions

## 5. American comic B&W / 美漫黑白

**Global style statement**
Black-and-white American comic-book illustration with heavy inks, crosshatching, and high-contrast spot blacks. Cinematic 16:9 panels with dramatic low-key lighting. Faces are graphic, not photoreal; backgrounds use heavy architectural perspective and Ben-Day-style hatching where appropriate.

**Color palette**
- #FFFFFF — Paper white
- #0A0A0A — Ink black
- #8C8C8C — Mid grey (for spot blacks)
- #D9D9D9 — Light grey (wash)
- #C0392B — Single accent red (use sparingly, max one shot per scene)

**Do / Don't**
- Do: crosshatching, high contrast, noir lighting, one-color accent
- Don't: full color palettes, soft gradients, anime eyes

## 6. 写实动漫 (default — hyper-real anime)

**Global style statement**
Cinematic widescreen storyboard painted in a modern hyper-real anime style. Crisp black outlines 2-3 px wide kept consistent across every frame. Skin has subsurface scattering, visible pores, and subtle makeup; eyes are anime-shaped but with realistic iris detail and catchlights. Hair is rendered strand by strand. Three-point cinematic lighting with strong key, soft fill, and rim light. Clean color grading suited to short-video vertical delivery.

**Color palette**
- #F4ECD8 — Soft key light
- #111111 — Outline black
- #1A1A1A — Deep shadow
- #E63946 — Accent red (use sparingly)
- #6FA8DC — Cool fill

**Do / Don't**
- Do: keep outlines consistent 2-3 px, lock seed per character, reuse reference sheet.
- Don't: drift to chibi, drop outlines on close-ups, add cel-shading, switch style mid-story.

## 7. 数字真人 (photoreal digital human)

**Global style statement**
Photoreal digital-human render indistinguishable from a real photograph. Natural skin texture with pores, fine hair, subtle blemishes. Soft naturalistic lighting that matches the setting. Shallow depth of field with an 85mm-equivalent lens look. No anime outlines, no stylization, no cel-shading. Color graded for short-video vertical delivery with subtle film grain.

**Color palette**
- #F5E6D3 — Skin highlight
- #C8A582 — Skin midtone
- #5C3A21 — Hair brown
- #2B2B2B — Deep shadow
- #FFFFFF — Catchlight

**Do / Don't**
- Do: keep lighting direction consistent, use a clean background for face isolation.
- Don't: add anime outlines, swap to anime style mid-video, use flat lighting.

## 14. 3D 国风仙侠 (国漫 CG 次世代)

Source: `references/3d-guofeng-xianxia-prompts.md`, based on the 知乎 article by 智瞳AI深空.

**Global style statement**
High-end 3D Chinese fantasy / guofeng CG style: next-gen modeling, PBR materials, real cloth and hair simulation, volumetric light, HDR lighting, cinematic depth of field, and 8K detail. Characters keep realistic skin texture and clean, readable face edges. **Color palette**
- #F5F1E8 — Cloud-white robe
- #1B1B1B — Ink outline / shadow
- #C9A227 — Imperial gold
- #7FA7C7 — Mist blue
- #A23E48 — Vermilion accent

**Do / Don't**
- Do: 3D国风动漫, 次世代建模, PBR材质, 真实布料/发丝, 体积光, 电影级光影, 8K超清, 角色一致性.
